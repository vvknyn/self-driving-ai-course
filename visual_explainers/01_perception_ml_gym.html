💡 The Self-Driving Perception Dilemma: In highway vision, 98% of all image pixels are empty road or sky. With standard Cross-Entropy ($\gamma = 0$), the cumulative loss from millions of trivially easy road pixels completely overwhelms the tiny gradient from a single distant pedestrian, causing the network to ignore the pedestrian entirely. By setting $\gamma = 2.0$, when a road pixel is well-classified ($p_t = 0.95$), its gradient contribution is discounted by $(1 - 0.95)^2 = 0.0025$ ($400\times$ reduction!), focusing 99% of backpropagation compute onto the safety-critical obstacles.
