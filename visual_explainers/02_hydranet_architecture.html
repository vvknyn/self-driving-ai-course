In Uncertainty Mode, the network uses MITx Maximum Likelihood: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{tot}} = \sum \frac{1}{2\sigma_i^2}\mathcal{L}_i + \log\sigma_i$. The vehicle variance $\sigma^2$ increases automatically, scaling down its gradient so all heads train equally!
